### How to Choose the Right AI Video Tool

Choosing the right AI video tool depends on your budget, technical skill level, and content type. Here’s how I break it down:  

- **Beginners**: Go with **Pictory** or **Descript**. Both have intuitive interfaces and minimal learning curves.  
- **Intermediate Creators**: Try **Runway ML** or **Synthesia** for more advanced editing and animation features.  
- **Budget Matters**: Free tools like **Kapwing** or **Clideo** are great for basic edits, but they lack the polish of paid options.  

If you’re serious about scaling your YouTube channel, invest in a tool that integrates with your workflow. For example, if you use **Make.com** to automate your social media posts, pairing it with an AI video tool can save you hours a week.
