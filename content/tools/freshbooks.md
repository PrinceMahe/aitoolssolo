# FreshBooks — Full Review & Verdict

FreshBooks is one of the easiest ways for a freelancer or small service business to get paid, full stop. It's built around a single idea: you send an invoice, the client pays, and the money lands in your account — with as little bookkeeping friction as possible. It's not a full accounting suite, and it doesn't try to be, which is exactly why so many self-employed people love it.

**Verdict: 4.1/5.** Best for freelancers, consultants, and agencies that bill by the hour or project and want billing + expenses + basic reporting in one dead-simple cloud app. If you need deep accounting, inventory, or payroll, a fuller suite like QuickBooks or Xero is a better fit.

## What it is

FreshBooks is a cloud invoicing and accounting platform aimed at the self-employed and very small service businesses. It's been around since 2003, so it's a mature product with a loyal following — particularly among US and Canadian freelancers who want something that feels like it was designed for humans, not accountants.

Its core job is turning the work you do into invoices, tracking costs, and giving you a simple picture of whether you're making money. You won't find heavy inventory management or complex multi-entity accounting here; that's deliberately out of scope.

## How it works / key features

**Polished invoicing.** This is the heart of the product. Invoices are quick to build, branded, automatically numbered, and you can set up recurring billing for retainer clients. Online payments (card and bank transfer) are built in, and FreshBooks sends automatic payment reminders — fewer "just following up on that invoice" emails for you.

**Time tracking.** You (or your team) log time against projects, and billable hours roll straight into invoices. Ideal for anyone who charges by the hour. There's a desktop timer and a mobile app.

**Expense capture.** Snap a photo of a receipt and FreshBooks reads and files it. Mileage tracking is included too, which is a genuine time-saver around tax season.

**Mobile app.** Invoicing, estimates, expenses, and payment notifications all work well from a phone — the mobile app is one of the better ones in this category.

**Simple reporting.** Profit-and-loss, expenses by category, and outstanding-balance reports give you enough to know where you stand without needing an accountant to interpret them.

| Plus | ~$30/mo | Up to 50 clients, more features |

Prices checked October 2026 and may drift. FreshBooks routinely runs first-year discounts, so the effective price is often lower. There's a 30-day free trial, and it's billed one client tier up as you grow.

## Where it shines / where it falls short

**Strengths**
- **Pleasantly simple invoicing.** The fastest path from "work done" to "invoice sent" in this category.
- **Excellent mobile app.** Manage billing on the go properly.
- **Built-in online payments and payment reminders** — designed to get you paid faster, not just book entries.
- **Expense capture with receipt OCR and mileage** — genuinely useful around tax season.
- **Smooth time-tracking-to-invoice flow** for hourly workers.

**Weaknesses**
- **Limited accounting depth.** No full inventory, and multi-entity/heavy accounting is out of scope.
- **Client-count tiers** can feel restrictive as you grow (the jump between tiers is real).
- **Cost.** Priced per-client-tier, so it gets relatively expensive compared to flat-rate competitors as you scale.
- **Limited payroll** — basic payroll exists in some regions but isn't a strength.

## Best for / not for

Best for **freelancers**, **consultants**, **designers**, **agencies**, and **any service business** that bills clients and wants simple, clean money management without hiring a bookkeeper.

Not ideal for **retail/e-commerce** (inventory and POS are outside its core), **larger teams needing deep accounting or payroll**, or **price-sensitive businesses** that just need flat-rate bookkeeping and don't rely on invoicing as their heart.

## Verdict & rating

**Rating: 4.1/5.** FreshBooks earns this because it nails the one job most self-employed people actually care about — getting paid — with a genuinely pleasant, mobile-first experience and built-in payment follow-up that competitors overcomplicate. The mark-down from a 5 is purely the accounting depth it deliberately doesn't have: if your needs outgrow invoicing + expenses, you'll eventually hit its ceiling.

**Bottom line:** if you're a freelancer or small service business and your pain point is billing and getting paid, FreshBooks is one of the simplest, most complete options you can start with today. The 30-day trial is more than enough to tell whether its simplicity matches how you work.

## FAQ

### Q: Is FreshBooks good for freelancers?
Yes — it's one of the best options. It's designed specifically around freelancer invoicing, time tracking, expenses, and online payments, with an excellent mobile app. If you bill by the hour or per project, the time-tracking-to-invoice flow alone is worth it.

### Q: How much does FreshBooks cost?
FreshBooks starts around $17/month (billed annually) for the Lite tier, going up to ~$55/month for Premium. Custom pricing is available for larger teams, and there's a 30-day free trial. Prices are current as of October 2026.

### Q: What are the main differences between FreshBooks and QuickBooks?
FreshBooks is simpler and friendlier but lighter on accounting features; QuickBooks offers deeper accounting, payroll, and inventory but is more complex to learn and use. Freelancers usually prefer FreshBooks; businesses needing full accounting often choose QuickBooks.

### Q: Does FreshBooks do payroll?
FreshBooks offers basic payroll in some regions, but it's not a core strength. If payroll is a major need, a dedicated payroll service or a more complete accounting suite is usually the better route.
