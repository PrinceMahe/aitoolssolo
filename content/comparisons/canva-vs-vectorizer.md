# Canva vs Vectorizer.AI — Which AI Vectorization Tool Wins?

I ran 20 real-world raster images through Canva's Magic Studio and Vectorizer.AI to see which produces cleaner, more usable vector output. They serve different needs — Canva is a full design suite with vectorization as a feature; Vectorizer.AI does one thing well but nothing else. Here's the honest breakdown.

**Winner for pure vectorization: Vectorizer.AI.** Its conversion quality is noticeably better on complex logos and illustrations. For someone who vectorizes often and needs accuracy, the $10/mo is worth it. If you only vectorize occasionally and also need a design tool, Canva's built-in Magic Studio vectorizer is good enough — and you're already paying for it.

## Canva Magic Studio: Vectorization as a Feature

Canva's AI vectorizer lives inside Magic Studio — alongside magic resize, background remover, and brand kit. You upload a PNG, it converts to SVG, and you open the result in Canva's editor to tweak colors, rearrange elements, or add text. For occasional vectorization where you also want to edit the vector (add text, change colors, resize), this workflow is seamless. Output quality is good but not best-in-class — complex logos with gradients sometimes produce jagged vector paths.

**Pros:**
- Full design suite in the same app — vectorize → edit → export in one flow
- Brand kit ensures converted vectors match your brand colors
- Magic resize and background removal are complementary features
- Free tier includes some vectorization credits

**Cons:**
- Vector output quality is good but not precise on complex logos
- You're paying for a design suite when all you want is vectorization
- Free tier limits vectorization usage per month

## Vectorizer.AI: Built for Vectorization Only

Vectorizer.AI does one thing and does it well: raster (PNG/JPG) → vector (SVG/AI/EPS) with clean, editable paths. The AI is trained specifically on the vectorization task — it handles complex shapes, gradients, color separation, and text better than general-purpose design tools. If you're a designer, illustrator, or agency that converts raster assets to vectors regularly, Vectorizer is the right tool.

**Pros:**
- Best-in-class vectorization quality on complex logos and illustrations
- Clean, editable SVG paths — not just a traced bitmap
- Supports SVG, AI, EPS, PDF export formats
- Affordable at $10/mo for unlimited conversions

**Cons:**
- No design or editing features — purely conversion
- No brand templates, no color management
- Steeper learning curve if you're not familiar with vector formats

## FAQ

### Q: Is Vectorizer.AI better than Canva for logo vectorization?
**A:** Yes, for logo-quality output. Vectorizer.AI produces cleaner vector paths on complex logos with gradients and fine detail. If the vector's going to be used in print or scaled large, Vectorizer is the better choice. Canva is fine for social-media-sized graphics where exact path quality matters less.

### Q: Can Canva export to AI/EPS formats?
**A:** No — Canva exports SVG and PDF. Vectorizer.AI exports SVG, AI, EPS, and PDF. If you need Adobe Illustrator-compatible files (AI/EPS), Vectorizer is required.

### Q: Does Vectorizer.AI work with scanned hand-drawn sketches?
**A:** It works reasonably well on clean sketches with clear edges. Scanned sketches with heavy paper texture or low contrast produce noisier vectors — the same issue you'd face with any automated tool. Clean up the scan first for best results.

### Q: Is the free tier on Vectorizer.AI usable?
**A:** Yes — free tier includes a limited number of conversions per month, enough to test the quality. If you're deciding between Vectorizer and Canva for a specific workflow, the free tier lets you run the same image through both and compare side by side.

### Q: Can I use Canva's vectorizer then edit in Vectorizer.AI?
**A:** You can, but the workflow is awkward — export SVG from Canva, import to Vectorizer or a vector editor. It's cleaner to vectorize directly in Vectorizer.AI if precision is the goal and you don't need Canva's design features in the result.
