# Obsidian Copilot vs Rewind: AI Note Assistant or Total Recall?

These tools look adjacent but solve opposite problems. Obsidian Copilot puts an AI inside your notes to help you write and think. Rewind (now part of the "Limitless" ecosystem) records everything you do on your screen and lets you search your past. One helps you *produce*; the other helps you *remember*.

## Quick comparison

| | Obsidian Copilot | Rewind |
|---|---|---|
| Lives in | Obsidian notes app | OS-level (Mac/Win) |
| Core job | Chat with your notes + generate | Record & search your activity |
| Privacy model | Local/your-API-key options | Constant local recording |
| Pricing | One-time / sub for cloud models | Subscription |

## Where Obsidian Copilot wins

- **Working inside your knowledge base.** Ask questions across your vault; get answers grounded in *your* notes.
- **Writing assist.** Draft, summarize, rewrite without leaving the app.
- **Privacy flexibility.** Point it at a local model if you don't want data leaving your machine.

## Where Rewind wins

- **"What did I just look at?"** Forgot the URL from 3 tabs ago? Rewind finds it.
- **Meeting recall.** Reconstruct what was said without taking notes.
- **Cross-app memory.** Not locked to one tool — it sees everything.

## Solo-founder verdict

Pick **Obsidian Copilot** if your bottleneck is *producing* notes, articles, and decisions from what you already know. Pick **Rewind** if your bottleneck is *losing track* of what you did across a chaotic day. Most founders benefit more from Copilot first (it directly improves output); add Rewind later if context-switching is eating you alive.

## FAQ

**Does Rewind record sensitive data?** It runs locally and you control playback, but it does capture screen content — review the privacy settings before enabling.

**Can Obsidian Copilot use my own API key?** Yes — you can bring your own key or run a local model, which keeps costs and data under your control.

**Are these mutually exclusive?** No. They serve different layers (notes vs OS memory) and can coexist.
