# Adobe Firefly vs Topaz Photo AI: Which Actually Fixes Your Photos?

Firefly and Topaz solve different problems. Firefly is Adobe's generative AI — make something from a prompt. Topaz Photo AI is a repair shop — make your existing photos sharper, cleaner, and bigger. If you're a solopreneur doing your own visuals, knowing which you need saves money.

## Quick comparison

| | Adobe Firefly | Topaz Photo AI |
|---|---|---|
| Job | Generate images from text | Upscale, denoise, sharpen real photos |
| Best for | Mockups, social assets, concepts | Product photos, old scans, low-res fixes |
| Pricing | Included in Creative Cloud / credits | ~$199 one-time |
| Standalone? | Needs Adobe account | Yes (desktop app) |

## Where Firefly wins

- **Creating from nothing.** Need a hero image for a landing page? Describe it.
- **Brand-safe generation.** Trained on Adobe's licensed stock — safer for commercial use than scraped-model alternatives.
- **Embedded in Photoshop/Express.** If you already pay for CC, it's effectively free.

## Where Topaz wins

- **Rescuing real photos.** Blurry product shot? Noisy low-light image? Topaz is the industry standard for recovery.
- **One-time cost.** No subscription — buy once, own it.
- **Batch processing.** Fix a folder of product photos in one pass.

## Solo-founder verdict

They're not really competitors — they're complementary. If your pain is **generating** visuals, Firefly (or your CC sub) covers it. If your pain is **fixing** photos you already shot, Topaz pays for itself on the first product shoot. Most solo founders need Topaz occasionally and Firefly only if they're already in Adobe's ecosystem.

## FAQ

**Do I need both?** Only if you both generate concepts and repair real photos. Start with whichever matches your bottleneck.

**Is Topaz a subscription?** No — it's a one-time license, which is rare and worth noting for budget planning.

**Can Firefly replace a designer?** For rough concepts and social tiles, yes. For polished brand work, still pair with a human or Canva.
