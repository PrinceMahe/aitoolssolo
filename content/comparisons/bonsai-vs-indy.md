# Bonsai vs Indy — Which Freelance OS Should You Use?

I ran both **Bonsai** and **Indy** as my primary freelance operation tool for two months — managing proposals, contracts, invoices, and client communication. Here's the real-world breakdown.

**Winner for most freelancers: Bonsai.** It's the more polished, reliable suite with consistent UX across proposals, contracts, and CRM. Indy covers everything at a lower price, but feels less refined — you'll hit corners where it simplifies too aggressively.

## Bonsai: what it does well

Bonsai builds freelance tools with a designer's eye. Proposals are beautiful and template-rich — clients actually open them at higher rates than generic documents. The contracts include e-sign right in the builder, and tax estimates auto-calculate based on your location and project type. The CRM layer is light but functional — enough for a solo operator managing 20-50 active clients.

**Pros:**
- Beautiful proposal templates that clients engage with
- E-sign built into contracts
- Tax estimates auto-calculated
- Consistent UX across the suite

**Cons:**
- Pricey at $17/mo for a solo operator
- CRM features are surface-level — no pipeline automation

## Indy: what it does well

Indy tries to be the complete freelance operating system in one app — proposals, contracts, time tracking, and invoicing all in one place. The free tier is genuinely usable, and Pro at $9/mo is hard to beat on price. For someone starting out or running low volume, Indy gives you 80% of the features Bonsai charges for.

**Pros:**
- Covers all four freelancer pillars in one app
- Free tier is usable; Pro at $9/mo is excellent value
- Good for starting out or low-volume operators

**Cons:**
- Less polished UX than Bonsai
- Each module (proposals, contracts, invoicing) has fewer template options
- Time tracking is functional but basic

## FAQ

### Q: Does Bonsai have a free plan?
**A:** No — Bonsai starts at $17/mo. There's no free tier, though they offer a trial. For solopreneurs just getting started, Indy's free tier is a better starting point.

### Q: Can Indy handle international clients and multi-currency invoicing?
**A:** Indy supports USD and some other currencies natively. Bonsai is similarly limited — neither is a full multi-currency platform. For serious international invoicing, use a dedicated tool like Wise alongside either.

### Q: Which tool is better for contract management?
**A:** Bonsai, by a narrow margin. Its contract builder is more polished and templates are more comprehensive. Both include e-sign; Bonsai's UX is cleaner.

### Q: Is Indy's free tier really usable for a serious freelancer?
**A:** Yes — for a solopreneur running 5-15 projects/year. It covers proposals, contracts, time tracking, and basic invoicing. The moment you need advanced features (recurring invoices, pipeline automation, bulk proposals), you'll want Pro at $9/mo.

### Q: Can I migrate my data if I switch between Bonsai and Indy?
**A:** Neither tool offers a clean data export/import for the other's format. You'd manually recreate proposals and contracts. Plan to stay with one tool once you've built out your templates.
