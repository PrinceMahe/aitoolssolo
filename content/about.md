## Hey, I'm the person behind AI Tools Solo.

I'm a solopreneur who got tired of reading tool reviews written by people who clearly never used the thing they're reviewing. You know the ones — "Top 10 AI Writing Tools" where every single one gets a glowing 4.5/5 and an affiliate link.

That's not what this site is.

**I actually buy, test, and build with every tool I write about.** Sometimes for weeks. Sometimes I burn real money figuring out the pricing traps so you don't have to. When I say Make.com's free tier is a trap, it's because I spent $12K on automations to find out where the walls are. When I compare Descript vs Riverside, it's after editing 200+ podcast episodes across both.

### What this site is about

Real, field-tested reviews and comparisons of AI tools, automation platforms, and SaaS products — written for **people who work alone** and can't afford to waste time or money on the wrong tool.

I cover:

- **AI writing tools** — Jasper, ChatGPT, Claude, and whatever ships next week
- **Automation platforms** — Make.com, Zapier, n8n, and the no-code stack
- **Email & newsletters** — Beehiiv, ConvertKit, Substack, the whole landscape
- **Hosting & infrastructure** — Hostinger, Cloudflare, Vercel, and the budget tier reality
- **Podcast & video** — Descript, Riverside, and the editing tools that actually save time

### Who this is for

Solopreneurs, indie hackers, freelancers, one-person content teams, small agency owners — anyone building something without a team of 50 behind them. If you make your own tool decisions and pay for them out of your own pocket, this site is for you.

### The deal

No fluff. No filler. No "it depends" without telling you what I'd actually pick. Every post ends with a clear recommendation because you came here for an answer, not a hedge.

If I'm wrong about something, tell me. If there's a tool you want me to test, reach out.
